# Sales Performance Dashboard (Excel)

## Overview
Interactive sales dashboard built in Excel as part of the Advanced Data Analysis course at NTI. It summarizes revenue by product category, region, sales representative, and month.

## Dataset
`sales_data.csv`: raw sales data (sales amount, product category, region, sales rep, date, units, discount).

## Dashboard Features
- Title and KPI cards: Total Revenue, Total Units Sold, Average Order Value, Average Discount
- Slicers: Product Category, Region
- Charts: Revenue Share by Category, Revenue by Region, Revenue by Sales Rep, Monthly Revenue Trend
- Filter button

## Tools
Microsoft Excel (Pivot Tables, Pivot Charts, Slicers)

## Key Insights
- Total revenue reached $5.02M from 25,355 units sold, with an average order value of $5,016 and an average discount of 15.2%.
- Revenue is split almost evenly across categories: Clothing 26%, Furniture 25%, Electronics 25%, Food 24%. No single category dominates.
- David is the top sales rep by revenue, followed by Bob. Charlie has the lowest revenue.
- Monthly revenue fluctuated through 2023, with the highest months at the end of the year (October and November).
- The sharp drop in January 2024 is likely due to incomplete data for that month, not a real decline.

## Recommendations
- Study what David and Bob do differently and share their practices with the rest of the team.
- Prepare inventory and campaigns ahead of the Q4 peak.
- Since the category mix is balanced, look at profit margin, not only revenue, to decide where to focus.
